# Memory Items

## Engine Failure

### Engine failure during takeoff

#### Below V1

1. Brakes -- apply
2. Thrust levers -- idle

#### Above V1

1. VR -- rotate to target pitch attitude
2. Climb V2
3. Positive rate -- gear up
4. 1500' AGL / obstacle clear
5. Accelerate V2 + 25 -- flaps up
5. Accelerate 200 KIAS

### Engine failure during approach

1. CWS master switch -- press and release
2. Operative engine thrust as required
3. Flaps 20
4. VREF + 5

## Engine Fire Voice Activates

### On the affected engine

1. Thrust -- IDLE
2. If fire continue more than 15 seconds or there are other indications
    1. Thrust -- CUTOFF
    2. Fire Switch (lift guard) -- FIRE PUSH
    3. Extinguisher #1 (lift guard) -- ARMED

![Learjet 45 Engine/Fuel/DEEC control panel.](/img/learjet-45/engine-fuel-deec-control-panel.png){width=460}

## Immediate engine airstart

1. Thrust -- IDLE
2. FUEL STBY switch -- ON

![Learjet 45 Engine/Fuel/DEEC control panel.](/img/learjet-45/engine-fuel-deec-control-panel.png){width=460}

## Emergency descent

* "Cabin altitude" voice activates
* Cabin altitude (if applicable)
* Cabin alt (CWP) (if applicable)
* Or cabin altitude exceeds 10,000 ft.

1. Crew oxygen masks -- don (100% oxygen)
2. Thrust -- IDLE
3. Autopilot -- disengage
4. SPOILER lever -- EXT
5. Descend VMO/MMO (not below minimum safe altitude)
6. PAX OXYGEN DEPLOY (lift guard) -- ON
7. MIC/MASK switch -- MASK
8. INPH switch -- INPH

![Learjet 45 pressurization panel.](/img/learjet-45/pressurization-panel.png){width=460}

## Cabin / cockpit smoke, fire, or fumes

1. Crew oxygen masks -- don (100% oxygen)
2. Smoke goggles -- don
3. EMER DEPRESS (lift guard) -- ON
4. MIC/MASK switch -- MASK
5. INPH switch -- INPH

## "Overspeed" voice activates

* Recovery from inadvertant overspeed

1. Thrust levers -- IDLE
2. Autopilot -- disengage
3. SPOILER lever -- EXT
4. Identify aircraft pitch and roll attitude
5. Level wings
6. Elevator and pitch trim -- as required to raise the nose

## Control Jam

### Elevator control jam

1. Attitude control -- as required
    1. User pitch trim and thrust as necessary
2. ELEV DISC handle -- pull and rotate (ELEVATOR DISC white CAS)
    1. The pilot will control the left elevator and the copilot will control the right
3. Determine elevator without the jam and transfer control to that pilot
    1. Do not apply additional force to the jammed control
    2. Do not use PIT TRIM BIAS

![Learjet 45 forward pedestal controls.](/img/learjet-45/forward-pedestal-controls.png){width=460}

### Aileron control jam

1. Attitude control -- as required
    1. User rudder and thrust as necessary
2. ROLL DISC lever -- PULL (ROLL DISC white CAS)
    1. Pilot -- control aircraft with spoilerons
    2. Do not use copilot control
    3. Do not use AIL TRIM

![Learjet 45 pilot control wheel switches.](/img/learjet-45/pilot-control-wheel-switches.png){width=460}

### Rudder control jam

1. Attitude control -- as required
    1. User roll control and thrust as necessary

## Uncommanded Motion

### Pitch axis uncommanded motion

1. Control wheel master switch (MSW) -- depress and hold
2. Attitude control -- as required
3. Thrust levers -- as required

![Learjet 45 pilot control wheel switches.](/img/learjet-45/pilot-control-wheel-switches.png){width=460}

### Roll or yaw axis uncommanded motion

1. Control wheel master switch (MSW) -- depress and hold
2. Attitude control -- as required

![Learjet 45 pilot control wheel switches.](/img/learjet-45/pilot-control-wheel-switches.png){width=460}

## Loss of both generators and both main batteries

1. L and R BATT switches -- ON one at a time
2. L and R GEN circuit breakers -- Set
3. Engine N2 -- 80% or above
4. L and R GEN switches -- one at a time, depress for approximately 1 second

## Emergency braking

1. EMERGENCY / PARKING BRAKE handle - pull smoothly
    1. EMER / PARK BRK white CAS message

## Emergency Evacuation

1. EMERGENCY / PARKING BRAKE -- Set
2. Thrust levers -- CUTOFF
3. EMER LIGHTS switch -- ON
4. EMER BATT L and R BATT switches -- OFF
