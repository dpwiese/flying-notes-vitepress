# My Questions

* What kind of engines?
  * AlliedSignal TFE-731-20 turbofan engines.
  * Each engine is rated at 3500 lb thrust at sea level.
* What kind of APU?
  * Allied Signal Model RE100 [LJ] APU
* Where does APU fuel come from?
  * Fuel for the APU flows from the right wing fuel tank through the right standby pump, a shutoff valve, and a fuel filter prior to reaching the engine.
* How much fuel does the APU use?
  * The APU uses approximately 150 lb of fuel per hour.
* How many batteries are there?
  * Two main batteries one emergency battery
* What is the AC electrical system used for?
  * The AC power generation system provides AC electrical power to the aircraft for windshield heating.
* What provides AC power?
  * AC electrical power is provided by two engine-driven alternators.
* What provides DC power?
  * Primary aircraft DC electrical power is provided by two engine-driven starter/generators.
* How many volts do the generators supply?
  * During normal operation, these generators supply 28 VDC power to the electrical system.
  * The starter/generators turn the engines for starting and then supply 28-VDC power to the aircraft when the engines are at operating speed.
  * Each starter/generator is rated at 30 VDC and 400 amp in a speed range of 6800 through 12,000 rpm.
