### Lights

### Fire Detection

* Master Warn tone and light
* All fire / extinguisher lights on switches on engine panel illuminate
* Red FIRE messages in ITTs flash
* L and R BLEED AIR LEAK red CAS
* WING/STAB LEAK red CAS
* APU FIRE switch and APU FIRE red CAS illuminate
  * APU master needs to be on for this

### Gear

* Master warn tone and light
* "Gear" audible voice
* GEAR red CAS

### Flaps

* FLAPS FAIL amber CAS
* FLAPS FAULT amber CAS
* Master caution tone and light activates for 5 seconds
* Amber flap synoptic
  * The box around the flap setting on the Summary page

### Stall Warn

* AOA sweeps to 1.0
* "Stall" audible voice
* Stick shaker
* Left and then right side

### Anti-Ice
